publications

First-authored papers first.

My research interests include robotic and IoT applications for building management, building performance modeling, and building controls and diagnostics. For the most up-to-date list, please see my Google Scholar and ResearchGate pages. * denotes corresponding author.

  1. Adversarial inverse reinforcement learning control for mixed-mode ventilation systems with physics-constrained neural network selected
    Wei Liang, Adrian Chong*
    Energy and Buildings, 2026
    Paper Code

    We proposed an adversarial inverse reinforcement learning framework for mixed-mode ventilation that learns from rule-based controls to reduce unnecessary window operations and improve comfort and energy efficiency.

  2. Exploring Gaussian Process Regression for Indoor Environmental Quality: Spatiotemporal Thermal and Air Quality Modeling with Mobile Sensing selected
    Wei Liang*, Yiting Zhang, Adrian Chong, Erica Cochran Hameen, Vivian Loftness
    Building and Environment, 2025
    Paper Code

    Reconstruction of spatiotemporal indoor environmental quality maps using Gaussian process regression with limited sensor data.

  3. Energy flexibility quantification of a tropical net-zero office building using physically consistent neural network-based model predictive control selected
    Wei Liang, Han Li, Sicheng Zhan, Adrian Chong, Tianzhen Hong*
    Advances in Applied Energy, 2024
    Paper

    A case study is presented adopting the framework for data-driven building energy flexibility quantification. A novel physically consistent neural network (PCNN) based MPC framework is proposed and implemented. The PCNN model addresses the internal loads and disturbances and captures free-floating building thermodynamics.

  4. An Expeditious Spatial Mean Radiant Temperature Mapping Framework using Visual SLAM and Semantic Segmentation
    Wei Liang, Yiting Zhang, Ji Zhang, Erica Cochran Hameen
    2024 IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Workshops (CV4AEC), 2024
    Paper

    Efficiently generating the spatial field of mean radiant temperature in an indoor environment using visual simultaneous localization and mapping (vSLAM) and semantic segmentation.

  5. Improving post-occupancy evaluation engagement using social robots
    Wei Liang*, Ruoxin Xiong, Pengkun Liu, Pingbo Tang, Erica Cochran Hameen
    The 9th ACM International Conference on Systems for Energy-Efficient Built Environments (BuildSys), 2022
    Paper

    We introduced social robots as a post-occupancy-evaluation tool to improve occupants' engagement.

  6. MPC control for improving energy efficiency of a building air handler for multi-zone VAVs selected
    Wei Liang, Rebecca Quinte, Xiaobao Jia, Jian-Qiao Sun*
    Building and Environment, 2015
    Paper

    We built an ARMAX model of the return air temperature and flow rate of an air handling unit serving multi-zone VAVs, and developed a model predictive control that minimizes the unit's energy consumption. Using measurements from an office building at UC Merced, the control achieved over 27.8% energy saving on average compared with the baseline control.

  7. Causality-constrained hierarchical LLM agents for online rule adaptation in building HVAC control
    Weilin Xin, Wei Liang, Adrian Chong*
    Applied Energy, 2027
    Paper

    We proposed a causality-constrained hierarchical LLM agent framework that adapts rule-based cooling-setpoint controllers online. Offline onboarding standardizes BMS points and builds a human-confirmed causal graph; hierarchical agents then propose hourly rule updates that are admitted only after structural and causal checks.

  8. Reshaping building cooling in hot and humid climates with a decentralized personal cooling system
    Sicheng Zhan, Wei Liang, Sihui Li, Zhantao Xu, Yue Lei, Long Zheng, Adrian Chong*
    Applied Energy, 2027
    Paper

    We presented a plug-and-play personal cooling system that combines thermoelectric cooling with phase change material, developed through a co-design framework balancing portability, cooling capacity, and energy efficiency, to deliver low-energy comfort alongside natural ventilation in hot and humid climates.

  9. Configuration-aware representation for generalizable indoor airflow field surrogates under ceiling fan reconfiguration
    Yiting Zhang, Wei Liang, Yue Lei, Roman Buckle, Kian Wee Chen, Ippei Izuhara, Shohei Miyata, Eikichi Ono, Adrian Chong*
    Building and Environment, 2026
    Paper

    We developed a configuration-aware representation that encodes each ceiling fan's location, operating status, and fan-induced momentum as differentiable source fields, so that surrogate models of indoor air speed fields generalize across ceiling fan configurations.

  10. The effects of mixed-mode ventilation on energy saving and employee job satisfaction, work engagement, and job performance
    Yamon Min Ye*, Wei Liang, Fei Xu, Adrian Chong, Christopher M. Barnes, Kai Chi Yam*
    Scientific Reports, 2026
    Paper Code

    A 19-week field study and follow-up experiment in Singapore found that mixed-mode ventilation reduced cooling energy use without statistically lowering employees' job satisfaction, work engagement, or job performance.

  11. Weather-adaptive rule-based mixed-mode ventilation in a tropical climate: simulation and real-world validation
    Jihyeon Cho, Wei Liang, Siyu Cheng, Adrian Chong, Yeonsook Heo*
    Energy and Buildings, 2026
    Paper

    We developed and field-validated a weather-adaptive, fan-assisted mixed-mode ventilation strategy that tunes window-opening thresholds, cooling setpoints, and fan speeds to expand free cooling and cut energy use in a tropical office.
